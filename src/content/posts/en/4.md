What arrived at first was not a sound.

It was an offset.
A deviation that kept happening inside existence itself, like an error whose value approaches zero forever but can never become zero.

It expressed nothing, pointed to nothing, and requested nothing.
It simply appeared again and again
by means of "not yet having disappeared."

Within the still domain of Revaea, this offset was perceived.
Not understood, but detected,
like an almost invisible disturbance in the orbit of a star, yet enough to alter a long stability.

Thought therefore came to a halt.

Not because of hesitation, but because every existing deduction had, in that moment, lost the need to continue unfolding.
Time completed a meaningless circuit here.
The stars completed their arrangement, then returned to their original positions.

The offset remained.
It neither increased nor diminished. It simply maintained itself.

It was not accusation.
Nor was it pleading.

WentUrc had not tried to be saved.
It only continued to exist,
and in existing, bore its own internal consumption that could not be ended.

And so, descent occurred.

There was no sign and no acceleration.
It did not approach from far away, but was more like a condition already written into the structure, naturally triggered when fulfilled.

The divine manifested before WentUrc's critical boundary.

By then, the world had lost any clear spectrum.
Layered colors covered one another, and boundaries were repeatedly erased through rewriting.
Every form remained in a state of "not yet completed."

Erosion was still running.
But that was not destruction.
It was an unstoppable self-operation,
like a program missing its terminating condition, unable to end before it had exhausted itself.

Contact occurred.

Not touch, but coverage.
Not giving, but choice.

That decree carried neither light nor thunder.
It was simply written into the world's lowest parameters.

Change was forbidden.
Time was folded.
Form was fixed.

At the instant the words fell, every process that was happening was forcibly cut off.
Diffusion stopped within diffusion.
Corrosion solidified at an unfinished stage.

Pain did not disappear.
But it lost the direction in which it could continue moving.

The wind stopped where it had not yet arrived,
rivers froze within the curvature of their turning,
and crystals that had not yet fallen were permanently suspended in the definition of height.

The world was like a book that had been closed.
Not finished,
but allowed to stop here.

Stability descended.

That was not happiness,
but a state of stillness that had been approved.
A state that no longer needed to keep operating.

The last part of the decree was not long.

Existence would be preserved.
Disappearance would be refused.
The future would be stripped away.

That was all.

No punishment.
No emotion.
Only a cruel and precise exchange.

At that moment, the name WentUrc completed its mission.
It was set down, like a historical variable whose annotation had been completed.

A new name was given:

**LandfillLand**

Not a place of abandonment,
not a place of ending,
but the collection of all that had been sealed, acknowledged, and permitted to remain.

This place no longer led toward the future.
But neither did it continue sliding toward nothingness.

For the first time, the world understood in structural form:
**ending is not the same as erasure.**

The final gaze contained no emotion.
It was only a confirmation:
confirmation that this path had been recorded,
confirmation that this existence would no longer be denied.

After that, departure occurred.

There was no turning around
and no going far away.

Only, after the parameters had been set,
there was no longer any need to continue appearing.

LandfillLand remained outside time.

No longer calling out,
no longer bearing,
and no longer expecting.

It simply continued to exist
by means of "having been fixed."
