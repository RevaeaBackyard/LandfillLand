When time began to flow again,
LandfillLand was not brought back into its trajectory.

It remained at the instant when that cutting-off occurred,
like a wedge embedded into the lowest layer of the world,
no longer advancing and impossible to remove.

There was no alternation of day and night here,
and no returning of seasons.
Light stayed at the angle of its arrival,
shadows kept their unfinished forms,
and every change was preserved before it could happen.

LandfillLand no longer generated.
But it was not empty.

It preserved every state that had already occurred:
uncovered and unupdated.

The wind stayed at the critical point just before scattering petals,
melody froze at the pitch closest to overflowing,
and leaves that had not yet fallen were held up by time,
suspended in the position of "about to happen."

No life walked here,
yet everywhere were structural traces left behind by life.

LandfillLand was not visited often.
It did not need to be returned to.

But every time the world looked back at its own history,
the earliest records of vibration would always be retrieved here:
those parameters not yet corrected by order,
those tremors not yet named.

What was sealed here was not error,
nor a sample of failure.
What it preserved was:
the evidence that existence had once unfolded freely
before everything was classified.

Therefore, LandfillLand became a vehicle.

Not a tool that leads to the future,
but a structure that carries the past.
Not for the sake of arriving,
but for preserving the traces of what had once moved.

Revaea and LandfillLand were preserved together,
like two directions of the world.

One continues forward,
carrying conjecture, order, and what has not yet happened.
One continues downward,
holding up memory, completion, and what has already ended.

They are not a hierarchy,
nor an opposition of better and worse.
They are the two ends required for wholeness.

In Revaea,
the world lifts its head to search for direction.
In LandfillLand,
the world echoes softly, marking where it came from.

If one day,
a traveler stands at the edge of the world
and hears beneath their feet something like wind,
or something like the echo of a residual melody:

that is not a hallucination,
and not a summons.

It is only LandfillLand,
continuing to prove one thing
in its own mode of existence:

some things do not need to continue,
but they should not be erased.

Therefore, moving forward is permitted.
Therefore, the future can be established.

The story is completed here,
but it has not been deleted.

Because at the deepest layer of the world,
there is a bearing surface that will never collapse.
It preserves the past
and makes room for the future.

Its name is:
**LandfillLand**
