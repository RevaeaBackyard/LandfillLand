IGCyukito has never been a stranger.
She is actually that Yukira who laughs freely and radiantly beneath the sun:
only when your gaze truly falls on her, she will suddenly blush, gently swallow back the words she has not yet said, and reveal a little shyness she cannot quite hide.

You must have seen that energetic Yukira, haven't you?
She will pull you running through the crowd, her voice clear and bright, swaying in the air like wind chimes.
But when you turn back, perhaps you will find Yukito:
that small figure who, after the liveliness has faded, quietly tugs at the hem of your clothes and hides behind you;
a timid one who does not quite dare to meet a stranger's eyes, yet is willing to give all her trust to you.

She wants to be affectionate with you, but is too embarrassed to speak.
So she can only hug her knees, bury her face in her scarf,
and reveal a pair of damp, wide eyes, secretly, secretly looking at you.
Like a little soft candy about to melt.

She does not have so much endless courage to embrace the entire world.
So she saves up all her courage little by little:
only so that, in a quiet corner,
with that soft and gentle voice only you can hear,
she can quietly call your name.
