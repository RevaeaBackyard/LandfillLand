## ❄ Archive Record

### 💠 IGCrystal

> A crystallized form of purity, reason, and the will of light.
>
> Meaning of the name: purity; reason; calm.
>
> IGCrystal is the maintainer of this world. Her power is comparable to that of a god, and she also possesses divinity.

> [!NOTE]
> Although IGCrystal has different names and different modes of appearance, this does not mean that she has any real-world psychological illness. Those names are more like facets she reveals in different states: for example, IGCyukira is her livelier and brighter side, while IGCyukito is quieter and more inward. Her occasional whitening, darkening, and other changes are also not simply repeated shifts of personality, but are affected by some kind of cyclical mechanism.
>
> In Revaea, the clarity and peace on the surface do not exist out of nowhere. Behind the beauty of that flawless far shore is actually a heavy and hidden cost: IGCrystal has always borne the duty of purifying the world. She stands quietly in the deepest place of the world, using her own soul as a vessel, continuously absorbing and filtering the nightmares and sorrow that drift at the edge of dreams. Whether it is the confusion and pain accumulated by living beings in daily life, or the vast despair left behind by the old world, all of it is ultimately received by her alone.
>
> For this reason, her state of mind inevitably fluctuates through the long cycle: at times it is clear as first snow, and at times it is covered by heavy shadow. That does not mean she has become fragmented. It is that, in order to protect the purity of this world, she must bear darkness for it again and again.
>
> As for why she is willing to do this, she only says:
>
> "I just do not want to see sorrow and pain spread across the whole world anymore."

### 🌨 English System

| Name | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Ice Glycoside Crystal** | The formal English translation, corresponding to the Japanese **雪グリコシド結晶**. This is the full English name. |
| **IGCrystal** | A shortened form. In the setting, it is the *husk* of *IGCyukira*. It is used for codenames, documents, and visual presentation. |
| **IGCyukira (outgoing and lively) / IGCyukito (inward and cute)** | Poetic nickname forms, built from *IGCrystal + Yukira / Yukito*, carrying personified and gentle qualities. They correspond to the Japanese forms. In the setting, they are the core of *IGCrystal*. |

> [!NOTE]

#### **1. Ending and Rebirth**

> $ending \iff \exists \Delta vars:\; reboot(hope, \Delta vars)$
>
> $reboot(hope, \Delta vars) \implies (tragiclys := \varnothing) \wedge (igcrystal := restored)$

- $ending \iff \exists \Delta vars:\; reboot(hope, \Delta vars)$

  It is already the ending, yet certain variables $\Delta vars$ still exist. They allow everything to be restarted and reach a new beginning.

- $reboot(hope, \Delta vars) \implies (tragiclys := \varnothing) \wedge (igcrystal := restored)$

  The power of hope and those variables is so great that **TragicLys fades away** and **IGCrystal is reborn**.

> 💡 A true ending may be unbearably sorrowful, but there is always hope that will save the one who has lost the way.

#### **2. The Fallen Ending**

> (igcrystal <= tragiclys) == ending

- `igcrystal <= tragiclys`

  This means that IGCrystal herself may, under certain conditions, be covered or affected by TragicLys.
- `== ending`

  This means that when this relation is fulfilled, the ending of the story or worldview is triggered.

> 💡 Life is not smooth from beginning to end. Will one keep falling, or continue to struggle?
