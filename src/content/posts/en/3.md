After the divine gaze moved away,
WentUrc did not immediately notice anything different.

The sky was still gorgeous,
colors like light that had not yet cooled
flowing slowly in the heights;
the land was still breathing,
rising and falling like a chest in sleep;
miracles were still happening every day,
as if nothing had changed.

Only,
some things began to slow.

The fissures that would once have been smoothed over by new thoughts
stayed where they were for the first time.
They did not continue to widen,
but neither did they disappear,
like wounds that had not yet healed,
touched again and again by time.

Time began to reveal its weight inside WentUrc.
It was no longer merely passing.
It was piling up.

Yesterday's miracles
were no longer gently covered by today's wishes.
Instead, they remained in the world as afterimages,
like faded patches of light,
layered over new scenes.

A mountain once raised by joy
failed to wait for the next wish
that would give meaning to its height.

And so,
it began to sink.

Not collapsing,
but slowly,
almost shamefully,
bending low,
as if realizing
that it was no longer needed.

Erosion was not a sudden disaster.
It was more like
a fatigue no one listened to.

When thought lost its answer,
miracle lost its meaning.

Those things that had only needed to exist briefly
were forced to prolong their lives,
yet did not know
how to keep breathing.

Magic began to stagnate.
Flowing imagination
congealed into crystals that could not dissolve,
hovering in the air
like frozen thoughts,
falling slowly and heavily.

When they fell,
they made no sound,
but across the land
they left echoes that would not disperse.

For the first time, the wind carried a sting.
It was no longer only a change in direction,
but more like the world trying to remind itself:
it was still alive.

The wind swept through valleys
with a dry, cracking sound;
it crossed empty fields
and carried away warmth that had not yet cooled.

WentUrc felt pain.

It was not tearing
and not destruction,
but a continuous,
uncomprehended ache.

Like light that must bloom forever,
yet can no longer wait for
the eyes that would look at it.

It did not know what it had done wrong.
It was only
existing in the first way it had been allowed to exist.

Flowers still bloomed,
but they could no longer wither naturally.
They were trapped in their most beautiful instant,
day after day,
until color gradually became hollow
and fragrance became a residue of memory.

Rivers continued to flow,
but forgot their destination.
Water circled in place,
turning back again and again,
echoing softly,
as if asking
a question no one would answer.

WentUrc did not resent Revaea.
It could feel
that perfection was not betrayal.

It was only envy.

It envied that world
which was still being listened to,
still being arranged,
still being allowed to have a future.

Erosion began spreading from the edges.
Not devouring,
but covering.

Like dust,
slow and gentle,
falling on the surface of light,
falling into the seams of memory,
and no matter what,
it could not be brushed away.

Every inch of land
trembled in a low voice.
Not from fear,
but from exhaustion.

"If only I could stop."
WentUrc thought this
among countless echoes.

"If beauty
did not have to continue forever,
perhaps I would not
hurt so much."

This was the first time
it longed for change.

Not to become stronger,
but to
end the pain.

In that long and silent time,
WentUrc learned something
it had never learned before:

**endurance.**

It carried
all its miracles that had not yet dispersed,
and waited quietly.

Waiting for some response,
waiting for some permission,
waiting for an ending
that could give a name
to "chaotic beauty."

And in the deepest place of the world,
that thought which could not be sent out
finally crossed the distance,
passed through layer upon layer of accumulated time,
and lightly
touched
the divine's awareness.
